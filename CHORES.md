# Chore To-Do List

Realistic timings to keep you honest. Most chores take longer than you think — but each one is a small win. Stack the wins.

---

## Quick wins (under 10 min each)

These give you momentum. Knock one out before you sit down.

- [ ] **Make the bed** — 2 min
- [ ] **Take out the trash / recycling** — 5 min
- [ ] **Wipe kitchen counters** — 5 min
- [ ] **Empty/load the dishwasher** — 8 min
- [ ] **Sort today's mail / inbox triage** — 10 min

> Five wins in under 30 minutes. Pick three and start.

---

## Self-care (you, but maintained)

- [ ] **Shower** — 12 min *(not 5. budget honestly: undress, wash, dry, dress)*
- [ ] **Skincare / teeth / grooming** — 8 min
- [ ] **Workout** — 45 min *(5 warm-up + 30 work + 10 cool-down/stretch)*
  - Quick option: 20 min HIIT / walk
- [ ] **Stretch or mobility** — 10 min

> Energy in = energy out. Do the workout *before* you cook — you'll feel earned, not drained.

---

## Kitchen (cook + bake)

- [ ] **Plan + grocery check** — 10 min
- [ ] **Cook a proper meal** — 45 min *(15 prep + 25 cook + 5 plate)*
  - Quick meal: 25 min
- [ ] **Bake** — 90 min *(20 mix + 30–45 bake + 20 cool + cleanup)*
  - Bread w/ rise: add 60–90 min hands-off
- [ ] **Wash up after cooking** — 15 min *(do it now, not tomorrow)*

> Pro tip: start the bake first — it runs in the background while you cook or iron.

---

## Laundry & clothes

- [ ] **Start a load of laundry** — 5 min hands-on *(60 min wash cycle)*
- [ ] **Hang / move to dryer** — 5 min
- [ ] **Fold + put away** — 15 min *(don't leave it on the chair)*
- [ ] **Iron** — 20 min for 4–5 items *(setup 3 + 4 min/shirt + cooldown)*
  - One shirt only: 8 min total

> Iron while a podcast plays. Time disappears.

---

## Home reset (weekly)

- [ ] **Tidy / declutter one room** — 15 min
- [ ] **Vacuum main areas** — 20 min
- [ ] **Mop kitchen + bathroom** — 20 min
- [ ] **Clean bathroom (sink, toilet, shower)** — 25 min
- [ ] **Change bedsheets** — 10 min
- [ ] **Water plants** — 5 min

---

## Admin (the boring but important)

- [ ] **Pay any due bills** — 10 min
- [ ] **Reply to 3 outstanding messages** — 15 min
- [ ] **Tomorrow's plan (3 priorities)** — 5 min

---

## Suggested running order for a productive day

1. Make bed (2) → dishwasher (8) → **10 min, already winning**
2. Workout (45) → shower (12) → **you've earned the day**
3. Start laundry (5) + start bake (20 hands-on) → **two timers running**
4. Iron while bake finishes (20)
5. Cook lunch/dinner (45) → wash up (15)
6. Tidy one room (15) → fold laundry (15)
7. Tomorrow's plan (5)

**Total active time: ~3h 15m** spread across the day. Feels like more because two things often run in parallel — that's the trick.

---

## Rules that actually work

1. **Double your gut estimate.** If you think 10 min, budget 20.
2. **Start the longest passive task first** (laundry, bake, oven preheat).
3. **One chore at a time** while it's active — multitasking inflates timings.
4. **Tick the box.** Visible progress = dopamine = momentum.
5. **Done > perfect.** A 70%-clean kitchen tonight beats a spotless one never.

> You don't need motivation. You need the next 2-minute task. Pick one above. Go.
